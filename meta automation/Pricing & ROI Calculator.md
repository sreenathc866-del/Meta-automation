## 🧮 Cost-Per-Customer (ROI) Formula
* **Daily Passengers per Autorickshaw:** 80 to 100 passengers enter each vehicle per day.
* **1 Month Calculation:**
  $$\frac{₹2,000}{30 \text{ days}} = ₹66.67/\text{day}$$
  $$\frac{₹66.67}{80 \text{ to } 100 \text{ passengers}} = ₹0.67 \text{ to } ₹0.83 \text{ per engaged view}$$
* **2 Months Calculation:**
  $$\frac{₹3,500}{60 \text{ days}} = ₹58.33/\text{day}$$
  $$\frac{₹58.33}{80 \text{ to } 100 \text{ passengers}} = ₹0.58 \text{ to } ₹0.72 \text{ per engaged view}$$
